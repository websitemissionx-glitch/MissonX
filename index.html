ABOUT US

MissionX, a hybrid word formed by merging "Innovation" and "Community," embodies our commitment to pioneering change within the community itself. We firmly believe that true innovation emerges from the hearts and minds of the community's own members. MissionX's visionary goal can be summed up as 10³ X 10⁶ > 10⁹

MissionX | Journey Watch on YouTube

FROM SOCIAL INNOVATION TO COMMUNITY CENTRIC EDUCATION

To build compassion among students towards the people of the society and to catalyse interaction between various stakeholders of the society, we experimented on various fronts. First we went to the fields with the students, then we brought the stakeholders to the college campus. Sometimes we had to hear students' parents yelling at us for sending them out during the college hours, while sometimes faculties complained about students not attending classes due to extra work given.

We endured every bit of it, and finally after 3 years we realised that there are about 4% of students who have an entrepreneurial mindset built in them due to various stages of life experiences they have had. We needed to find them and give them a track to help them explore, develop and build an entrepreneurial career. While the rest had to go through a curated journey that helps them build an entrepreneurial mindset. Thus, one program for all was evolved "the community centric education".

What is this program?

Think like a Designer. Build like an Innovator.

Innovation & Design Thinking is an experiential 12-week program that takes students through the five stages of design thinking—Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, and Test—applied to real community challenges. Students don't just learn theory; they visit communities, interview stakeholders, and build working prototypes that are validated with real users.

The program is facilitated by industry practitioners, academic mentors, and community leaders who co-create the learning journey with students—ensuring every insight is grounded in lived experience.

  • Hands-on design sprints every week
  • Industry expert guest sessions
  • Community immersion & field visits
  • Prototype & pitch to real stakeholders
The Framework

The 5 Stages of Design Thinking

Stage 01

Empathize

Understand the human needs behind the problem through interviews, observation & immersion.

Stage 02

Define

Synthesise research findings into a sharp, actionable problem statement.

Stage 03

Ideate

Generate hundreds of creative ideas through brainstorming, SCAMPER, and divergent thinking.

Stage 04

Prototype

Build rapid, low-cost prototypes to make ideas tangible and test assumptions quickly.

Stage 05

Test

Validate solutions with real users, gather feedback, and iterate toward a refined product.

Program Syllabus

01

Foundations of Design Thinking & Empathy

Introduction to human-centred design, empathy mapping, and stakeholder interviews to uncover unmet needs.

02

Problem Framing & Insight Synthesis

Translating raw observations into actionable insights using How Might We statements and affinity mapping.

03

Creative Ideation Techniques

SCAMPER, mind mapping, crazy-8s, and other divergent thinking tools to generate breakthrough ideas.

04

Rapid Prototyping & MVP Building

Paper prototypes, wireframes, and physical models—moving from concept to tangible artefact in hours.

05

User Testing & Iterative Refinement

Structured user testing protocols, feedback loops, and pivoting strategies to reach a validated solution.

06

Capstone: Pitch to Community Stakeholders

Teams present their final solution to a panel of community leaders, industry mentors, and investors.

Pre-requisites

Open to all streams. No prior design or tech knowledge required — curiosity and a willingness to learn is enough.

What we build

A Space Designed for Tinkerers, Builders & Dreamers.

A Makerspace is a collaborative, hands-on workspace where students can work on projects using tools ranging from soldering irons and 3D printers to laser cutters and CNC machines. MissionX designs these spaces end-to-end — from spatial planning and equipment procurement to faculty training and curriculum integration.

Every Makerspace we build is custom-scoped to the institution’s goals, student demographics, available space, and budget — ensuring maximum utilisation and measurable outcomes from day one.

  • Needs assessment & space audit
  • Equipment selection & layout design
  • Faculty training & onboarding
  • Ongoing program support & events
What’s Inside

Equipped for Every Kind of Maker

From digital fabrication to electronics and coding, every Makerspace is curated to inspire creativity across disciplines.

3D Printing Lab

FDM and resin printers that let students turn digital designs into physical objects within hours — accelerating prototyping cycles dramatically.

Electronics & IoT Station

Arduino, Raspberry Pi, sensors, and soldering benches for students diving into embedded systems, robotics, and smart device development.

Laser Cutting & CNC

Precision digital fabrication tools for cutting, engraving, and shaping wood, acrylic, and metal to manufacture real product enclosures and parts.

Software & Coding Hub

High-performance workstations with professional software for CAD, graphic design, game development, AI/ML, and web application building.

Design & Ideation Studio

Whiteboards, modular furniture, and maker kits designed for collaborative brainstorming, rapid sketching, and physical model-making sessions.

Media Production Corner

Podcast setup, camera, and lighting equipment so students can document, pitch, and communicate their innovations to wider audiences.
