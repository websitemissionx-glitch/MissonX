What is this program?

Think like a Designer. Build like an Innovator.

Innovation & Design Thinking is an experiential 12-week program that takes students through the five stages of design thinking—Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, and Test—applied to real community challenges. Students don't just learn theory; they visit communities, interview stakeholders, and build working prototypes that are validated with real users.

The program is facilitated by industry practitioners, academic mentors, and community leaders who co-create the learning journey with students—ensuring every insight is grounded in lived experience.

  • Hands-on design sprints every week
  • Industry expert guest sessions
  • Community immersion & field visits
  • Prototype & pitch to real stakeholders
The Framework

The 5 Stages of Design Thinking

Stage 01

Empathize

Understand the human needs behind the problem through interviews, observation & immersion.

Stage 02

Define

Synthesise research findings into a sharp, actionable problem statement.

Stage 03

Ideate

Generate hundreds of creative ideas through brainstorming, SCAMPER, and divergent thinking.

Stage 04

Prototype

Build rapid, low-cost prototypes to make ideas tangible and test assumptions quickly.

Stage 05

Test

Validate solutions with real users, gather feedback, and iterate toward a refined product.

Program Syllabus

01

Foundations of Design Thinking & Empathy

Introduction to human-centred design, empathy mapping, and stakeholder interviews to uncover unmet needs.

02

Problem Framing & Insight Synthesis

Translating raw observations into actionable insights using How Might We statements and affinity mapping.

03

Creative Ideation Techniques

SCAMPER, mind mapping, crazy-8s, and other divergent thinking tools to generate breakthrough ideas.

04

Rapid Prototyping & MVP Building

Paper prototypes, wireframes, and physical models—moving from concept to tangible artefact in hours.

05

User Testing & Iterative Refinement

Structured user testing protocols, feedback loops, and pivoting strategies to reach a validated solution.

06

Capstone: Pitch to Community Stakeholders

Teams present their final solution to a panel of community leaders, industry mentors, and investors.

Pre-requisites

Open to all streams. No prior design or tech knowledge required — curiosity and a willingness to learn is enough.
