What we build

A Space Designed for Tinkerers, Builders & Dreamers.

A Makerspace is a collaborative, hands-on workspace where students can work on projects using tools ranging from soldering irons and 3D printers to laser cutters and CNC machines. MissionX designs these spaces end-to-end — from spatial planning and equipment procurement to faculty training and curriculum integration.

Every Makerspace we build is custom-scoped to the institution’s goals, student demographics, available space, and budget — ensuring maximum utilisation and measurable outcomes from day one.

  • Needs assessment & space audit
  • Equipment selection & layout design
  • Faculty training & onboarding
  • Ongoing program support & events
What’s Inside

Equipped for Every Kind of Maker

From digital fabrication to electronics and coding, every Makerspace is curated to inspire creativity across disciplines.

3D Printing Lab

FDM and resin printers that let students turn digital designs into physical objects within hours — accelerating prototyping cycles dramatically.

Electronics & IoT Station

Arduino, Raspberry Pi, sensors, and soldering benches for students diving into embedded systems, robotics, and smart device development.

Laser Cutting & CNC

Precision digital fabrication tools for cutting, engraving, and shaping wood, acrylic, and metal to manufacture real product enclosures and parts.

Software & Coding Hub

High-performance workstations with professional software for CAD, graphic design, game development, AI/ML, and web application building.

Design & Ideation Studio

Whiteboards, modular furniture, and maker kits designed for collaborative brainstorming, rapid sketching, and physical model-making sessions.

Media Production Corner

Podcast setup, camera, and lighting equipment so students can document, pitch, and communicate their innovations to wider audiences.

Our Process

02

Design & Procurement

03

04

Launch & Ongoing Support
