FROM SOCIAL INNOVATION TO COMMUNITY CENTRIC EDUCATION

To build compassion among students towards the people of the society and to catalyse interaction between various stakeholders of the society, we experimented on various fronts. First we went to the fields with the students, then we brought the stakeholders to the college campus. Sometimes we had to hear students' parents yelling at us for sending them out during the college hours, while sometimes faculties complained about students not attending classes due to extra work given.

We endured every bit of it, and finally after 3 years we realised that there are about 4% of students who have an entrepreneurial mindset built in them due to various stages of life experiences they have had. We needed to find them and give them a track to help them explore, develop and build an entrepreneurial career. While the rest had to go through a curated journey that helps them build an entrepreneurial mindset. Thus, one program for all was evolved "the community centric education".

MISSION OF NURTURING ENTREPRENEURIAL MINDSET IN EVERY YOUTH.

Ever wondered how fostering an entrepreneurial mindset in today's youth can shape a brighter future for communities and nations?

At MissionX, our mission is crystal clear, we are dedicated to nurturing an entrepreneurial mindset in every young individual. We recognize the untapped potential within today's youth, and we firmly believe that fostering an entrepreneurial spirit is not just a pathway to personal success but also the key to unlocking innovation, economic growth, and societal progress.
