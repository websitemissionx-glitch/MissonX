First step towards becoming a change maker!

Social Innovation Program

One step at a time to

Empower Social change.

In this course students will be introduced to various sector specific, real time problems of the region. During the 12 weeks of the course, students go on multiple field visits to experience social immersion and develop compassion for their communities through continuous interactions. They simultaneously conduct primary research using the knowledge of research tools and methodologies. This is a completely activity based class, with a fun-loving environment that is infused with challenges.

Outcomes

of the program!

They are challenged to get out of their comfort zones and start engaging with the diverse sectors around the region.

Apply research methods

Apply research methods to organise, analyse and define the problem.

Design solutions

Design solutions to the challenges identified along with the stakeholders of the community.

Build rapid prototypes

Build prototype using rapid prototyping tools for the ideas that can solve the identified problem.

Social Innovation Program SYLLABUS!

01

INTRODUCTION TO REAL-TIME PROBLEM STATEMENT

Through hands-on activities, problem-solving exercises, and real-world case studies.

02

MARKET RESEARCH & PROBLEM ANALYSIS

In this topic students will learn various market research methodologies and techniques.

03

IDEA GENERATION & VALIDATION

Students will learn ideation techniques, market research methods, and best practices.

04

BUILDING BUSINESS MODEL FOR IDEA

Through lectures and creation exercises, students develop business model skills.

05

PROTOTYPING & USER VALIDATION

By creating prototypes and testing them with users, students develop user-centered design.

Pre-requisites

Prior knowledge of design thinking concepts and prototyping tools is required to take up this program.

Course Includes

  • 3 Case studies
  • 3 Expert Workshops
  • 2 Technical Events
  • 2 Field Visits
  • 1 Real-time project
  • Course Certificate
